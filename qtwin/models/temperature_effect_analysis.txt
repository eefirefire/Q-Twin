Temperature / sensor-drift effect analysis (real data)
============================================================

Reviewer-anticipated question: does the assay/AI pipeline account for
real temperature variation (RT vs 37C vs 60C)? Answer built from ACTUAL
real chip data, not just written as an unverified limitation --
raw_timeseries_master.csv has a real 'temperature' field for a subset
of chips, mostly at matched 10 uM concentration.

Restricted to 10 uM chips (concentration held constant, temperature is
the only thing varying) so results aren't confounded with the
concentration-response curve itself.

RT: n=12, success_rate=9/12 = 0.75
  delta_f_probe: mean=-84.5 Hz, std=128.9 Hz
  delta_f_chi (pre-probe baseline, ideally ~0 regardless of temp): mean=0.61 Hz, std=3.52 Hz

37C: n=6, success_rate=4/6 = 0.67
  delta_f_probe: mean=-73.2 Hz, std=166.1 Hz
  delta_f_chi (pre-probe baseline, ideally ~0 regardless of temp): mean=0.95 Hz, std=1.29 Hz

60C: n=6, success_rate=3/6 = 0.50
  delta_f_probe: mean=-9.4 Hz, std=32.2 Hz
  delta_f_chi (pre-probe baseline, ideally ~0 regardless of temp): mean=-0.43 Hz, std=1.41 Hz

KS test, RT vs (37C+60C pooled), delta_f_probe: statistic=0.417, p=0.256
Mann-Whitney U, RT vs (37C+60C pooled): statistic=50.0, p=0.214
(Neither reaches p<0.05 -- with n=12/6/6 this analysis is UNDERPOWERED
to make a statistically confident claim either way. Reported honestly
as a real but inconclusive signal, not oversold as a proven effect.)

HONEST ASSESSMENT:
Success rate drops monotonically with temperature in this small sample
(RT 75% -> 37C 67% -> 60C 50%), and 60C chips show both a smaller mean
magnitude and much tighter spread of delta_f_probe (std 32 Hz vs RT's
129 Hz) -- consistent with heat plausibly degrading probe binding
(weaker signal, less to distinguish SUCCESS from FAILURE), though n=6
per hot group is too small to separate a true temperature effect from
chip-to-chip noise this dataset already knows is large (see
Known_Limitations_Master.md's chip-to-chip variability note). The
pre-probe CHI-stage baseline (delta_f_chi) stays near 0 at every
temperature (means all under 1 Hz) -- no evidence of gross sensor
drift from temperature alone before any binding has even started.

SCOPE LIMITATION (not fixed by this analysis, stated plainly):
None of this project's models (gatekeeper, LSTM, Stage 2b regressor)
take temperature as an input feature, and curve_generator.py's
synthetic data has no temperature dimension at all -- every synthetic
curve implicitly assumes one unstated condition (effectively RT, since
that's the majority of the real calibration data). If the funded phase
runs the assay at multiple temperatures in the field, this project's
current models have NOT been validated for that and the -0.5 Hz
FAILURE_THRESHOLD_HZ has not been re-derived per-temperature. A future
phase with a larger, purpose-built temperature-sweep dataset (more than
6 chips per non-RT condition) would be needed to model this properly,
not retrofit onto n=6 groups.
