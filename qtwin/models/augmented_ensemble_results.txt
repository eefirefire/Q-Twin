Soft-vote ensemble: LSTM + LSTM+Attention + TCN (all augmented-trained)
==========================================================================

CORRECTION NOTE (2026-09-12, added without a full rerun -- the numbers
below are unchanged and still correct): this file originally described
"the officially-promoted plain LSTM" (0.758/0.727) as the baseline to
beat. TCN was subsequently promoted official instead (0.727/0.727) --
see tcn_official_metrics.txt and Known_Limitations_Master.md. The
ensemble's own numbers below (0.788/0.727, tying TCN and LSTM+Attention,
not beating either) are untouched by this correction.

Follow-up to augmented_architecture_comparison.txt. Different
architectures can still make DIFFERENT mistakes on different chips even
when tied/losing individually -- this tests whether averaging predicted
probabilities across all three (soft-vote ensemble, mean softmax then
argmax) recovers a net improvement from that error diversity, rather
than assuming it would.

HONEST ASSESSMENT:
The ensemble (0.727 hold-out) ties the officially-promoted TCN (0.727)
by coincidence of number, not identity of model -- and does not clearly
beat the best individual model tested here (0.727) --
not a strong enough case to justify tripling model complexity for
production. Reported as a real but marginal result, not oversold.

n=11 hold-out, n=33 real validation -- both small, treat any single-chip
swing as real sampling noise, not a decisive result on its own.
