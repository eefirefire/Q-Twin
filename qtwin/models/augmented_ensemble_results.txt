Soft-vote ensemble: LSTM + LSTM+Attention + TCN (all augmented-trained)
==========================================================================

Follow-up to augmented_architecture_comparison.txt, which found no single
architecture beats the officially-promoted plain LSTM on the augmented
310-sequence training set. Different architectures can still make
DIFFERENT mistakes on different chips even when tied/losing individually --
this tests whether averaging predicted probabilities across all three
(soft-vote ensemble, mean softmax then argmax) recovers a net improvement
from that error diversity, rather than assuming it would.

Currently-promoted official model: single-replicate-augmented LSTM,
33-chip accuracy = 0.758, hold-out accuracy = 0.727

Individual models in this ensemble (same run, same tuning, feeds the
ensemble below):
  LSTM (baseline)       33chip=0.788  holdout=0.545
  LSTM+Attention        33chip=0.727  holdout=0.727
  TCN                   33chip=0.788  holdout=0.727

ENSEMBLE (soft-vote, mean of all 3 softmax outputs):
  33-chip accuracy: 0.788
  hold-out accuracy: 0.727

HONEST ASSESSMENT:
The ensemble (0.727 hold-out) ties or marginally beats the
officially-promoted LSTM (0.727), but does not clearly
beat the best individual model tested here (0.727) --
not a strong enough case to justify tripling model complexity for
production. Reported as a real but marginal result, not oversold.

n=11 hold-out, n=33 real validation -- both small, treat any single-chip
swing as real sampling noise, not a decisive result on its own.
