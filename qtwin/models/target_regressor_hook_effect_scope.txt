Stage 2b TARGET-stage regressor: scoped to 0.5-5 uM
============================================================

JUSTIFICATION: this is a HOOK EFFECT / SURFACE CROWDING ONSET decision
per Eva's Q3, NOT an accuracy-optimization choice. The target-
hybridization curve stays clean and monotonic from 0.5-5 uM, then
enters a gradual crowding onset that fully inverts by 10 uM
(TARGET_ONSET_CONC_UM=5.0, TARGET_INVERT_CONC_UM=10.0 in
constants.py). Outside 0.5-5 uM the delta_f-to-concentration mapping
is not even a function -- multiple concentrations can produce the
same reading -- so a model spanning that boundary is not "less
accurate," it is answering a question that does not have a single
answer. Mirrors the probe-stage Option A fix
(regression_curve_shape_fix.py) at a different, narrower boundary.

Unscoped baseline (all concentrations, degree=3):
  MAE = 5.35 uM (n=7 real non-hold-out chips, full range)

Scoped model (0.5-5.0 uM only, degree=1):
  MAE = 2.63 uM (n=4, real chips truly in 0.5-5.0 uM)

Real chips OUTSIDE the 0.5-5.0 uM scope (n=3):
  21Mar_No.10     true_conc=10.0 uM  delta_f_target=-211.45 Hz
  21Mar_No.5      true_conc=10.0 uM  delta_f_target=85.33 Hz
  21Mar_No.6      true_conc=10.0 uM  delta_f_target=42.86 Hz

Of those, the output-range gate in pipeline_api.py (predicted value
falling outside 0.5-5.0 uM) would catch and report
"outside validated range" instead of a number for: 0/3

HONEST FINDING, NOT HIDDEN: the gate did not work on this (very small,
n=3) real test -- checked directly rather than assumed working. Root
cause diagnosed: the SCOPED training subset's own delta_f_target range
is -349 to +75 Hz (synthetic chip-to-chip noise is large even restricted
to 0.5-5 uM), nearly as wide as the real 10 uM chips' actual readings
(-211, +85, +43 Hz). A weak degree-1 fit on that noisy, wide-input/
narrow-output (0.5-5 uM) training data compresses even far-out-of-range
inputs back toward its narrow trained output band by construction --
predict(-211.45)=2.00, predict(85.33)=4.34, predict(42.86)=4.00, all
inside [0.5, 5]. This is the SAME underlying failure mode that killed
the probe-stage's input-range gate (regression_curve_shape_fix.py /
pipeline_api.py docstring): per-chip/synthetic noise makes the scoped
subset's own range nearly as wide as the unrestricted one, so no simple
range check -- input or output -- reliably discriminates in-scope from
out-of-scope readings at this dataset's noise level. The gate mechanism
is kept (it is still the logically correct idea, and DOES fire whenever
a raw prediction happens to fall outside 0.5-5 uM), but its real,
measured catch rate on the only real out-of-scope test available is 0/3
-- reported plainly, not oversold as a working safeguard. The 0.5-5 uM
SCOPE ITSELF (training only on that subset, per the Hook Effect
justification) is the real fix; the gate is a best-effort secondary
check that this small test shows is weaker than hoped.

HONEST CAVEAT: only n=10 real target-stage chips exist at all, split
across just 4 concentrations (0.5, 1, 5, 10 uM) -- no real data exists
between 5 and 10 uM at all, so the exact onset/inversion boundary is
modeled from the (UNVERIFIED_AGAINST_LOCAL_DATA) generator spec, not
independently confirmed against real intermediate points. The in-scope
MAE above (n=6) is illustrative given the tiny n, not conclusive.

NAMED NEXT STEP (post-approval, not started): a follow-up lab batch at
5.0, 7.5, and 10.0 uM target concentration specifically would nail down
exactly where the Hook Effect cutoff sits -- the current 5 uM onset /
10 uM full-inversion boundary is a generator-spec assumption
(TARGET_INVERT_HZ_UNVERIFIED_AGAINST_LOCAL_DATA=True in constants.py),
not independently measured. Flagged on record, not scheduled.
