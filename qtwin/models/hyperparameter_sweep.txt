CONCLUSION: 45s was chosen in Week 3 specifically because it's safely under
both the real minimum (49.99s) and the synthetic DURATION_MIN_S (50.0s) --
every curve, real or synthetic, has genuine measured/generated data across the
full window. A 60s window would need to drop or extrapolate at least one real
replicate (15Mar_No.17 replicate 2, 49.99s) -- a real coverage cost, not just a
marginal choice. A 30s window has full coverage too but throws away 15s of
curve shape the LSTM/attention models could otherwise use. 45s remains the
better-justified choice, confirmed by checking coverage directly rather than
assumed from the original Week 3 reasoning alone.
